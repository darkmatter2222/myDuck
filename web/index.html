DESIGN AN EXPERIMENT

Where should learning go next?

Training stages

Stages continue from the preceding stage’s final checkpoint. Generalization is an explicit experiment, not an automatic claim of walking ability.

Available skills: locomotion only. Gait = forward baseline; more progress = stronger displacement reward; explore = exploration preset; generalize = broader locomotion training. Sitting, standing up and recovery require new task definitions and validated rewards; they are not available policies yet.

Evaluation & unattended operation

A stall limit of 0 disables the learning-stall pause. Errors and low disk space still stop the job. Changing settings creates a new experiment.
